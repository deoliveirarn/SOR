6 The groups nu^q(G) and tau^q(G)

6.1 A polycyclic presentation of tau^q(G)

For a non-negative integer q, the group \nu^q(G) contains a normal subgroup \Delta^q(G) such that \tau^q(G) = \nu^q(G)/\Delta^q(G) \cong (G \wedge^q G) \rtimes (G \times G). For q = 0, \tau^0(G) = \nu(G)/\Delta(G) \cong \chi(G)/R(G).

Dias and Rocco [DR20] give a consistent polycyclic presentation of \tau^q(G) for a polycyclic group G, from a consistent polycyclic presentation g_1, \ldots, g_n of G and one of G \wedge^q G, the latter obtained from the q-central extension E_q(G) = F/R^q[R,F] as E_q(G)' E_q(G)^q. This package builds that presentation, starting from the Schur extension computed by the Polycyclic package.

The extension to a polycyclic presentation of \nu^q(G) itself, and hence of the q-tensor square G \otimes^q G, as the q-central extension \nu^q(G) = E_q(\tau^q(G)) [DR20, Section 7], is in preparation.

6.1-1 RoccoTauGroup
‣ RoccoTauGroup( G, q )( operation )

Returns: a pcp group

Returns \tau^q(G) = \nu^q(G)/\Delta^q(G) for a polycyclic group G (a finite solvable group in any representation, or a pcp group) and a non-negative integer q, as a pcp group given by the presentation of Dias and Rocco. Embedding(T, 1) and Embedding(T, 2) are the embeddings of G and G^\varphi, and RoccoExteriorSquareQ (6.1-2) is the normal subgroup isomorphic to G \wedge^q G.

6.1-2 RoccoExteriorSquareQ
‣ RoccoExteriorSquareQ( T )( attribute )

For T = \tau^q(G) returned by RoccoTauGroup (6.1-1), the subgroup W \cong G \wedge^q G.

gap> G := SymmetricGroup( 3 );;
gap> Size( RoccoTauGroup( G, 0 ) );   # = |chi(S_3)| / |R(S_3)|
108
gap> List( [ 0, 2, 3 ], q -> Size( RoccoExteriorSquareQ( RoccoTauGroup( G, q ) ) ) );
[ 3, 6, 6 ]
