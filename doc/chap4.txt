  [1X4.1 [33X[0;0YThe groups chi n(H) and chi(n,H)[133X[101X
  
  [33X[0;0YFor [23Xn[123X copies [23XH = H^{\psi_0}, H^{\psi_1}, \ldots, H^{\psi_{n-1}}[123X of a group [23XH[123X
  (Sidki, Rocco and de Oliveira [SRd26], with an appendix by D. Savchuk):[133X
  
  
  [24X[33X[0;6Y\chi_n(H)  = \langle H, H^{\psi_1}, \ldots, H^{\psi_{n-1}} \mid [h^{\psi_i},
  h^{\psi_j}] = 1 \ \forall h \in H,\ i \neq j \rangle,[133X
  
  [124X
  
  [33X[0;0Ythe "mother group", and its quotient[133X
  
  
  [24X[33X[0;6Y\chi(n,  H)  =  \langle \chi_n(H) \mid [D_{ij}, L_{kl}] = 1 \ \forall i \neq
  j,\ k \neq l \rangle,[133X
  
  [124X
  
  [33X[0;0Ywith  [23XD_{ij}  =  [H^{\psi_i},  H^{\psi_j}][123X  and [23XL_{ij} = \langle h^{-\psi_i}
  h^{\psi_j}  \mid h \in H \rangle[123X; the group [23X\chi(n, H)[123X was introduced in the
  doctoral  thesis  of  R.  N.  de  Oliveira  (2007).  The operator [23X\chi(n, -)[123X
  preserves  finiteness,  nilpotency  and  perfectness, while [23X\chi_n(H)[123X may be
  infinite  for  finite [23XH[123X (for instance [23X\chi_3(C_2 \times C_2)[123X). For a perfect
  group  [23XH[123X,  [23X\chi(n, H) \cong T_n(\tilde{H})[123X, a subquotient of [23X\tilde{H}^{n+1}[123X
  for  the  Schur  cover  [23X\tilde{H}[123X  of  [23XH[123X;  for [23Xn = 2[123X this is Sidki's theorem
  [23X\chi(H) \cong T(\tilde{H})[123X (1980, Theorem 4.4.1).[133X
  
  [1X4.1-1 SidkiChiNGroup[101X
  
  [33X[1;0Y[29X[2XSidkiChiNGroup[102X( [3XH[103X, [3Xn[103X ) [32X operation[133X
  [6XReturns:[106X  [33X[0;10Ya group[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YReturns  [23X\chi(n,  H)[123X for a finite [23Xp[123X-group or a finite perfect group [3XH[103X and an
  integer [3Xn[103X [23X\geq 2[123X. [10XEmbedding([110X[3XC[103X [10X, i)[110X, [23X1 \leq i \leq n[123X, is the embedding of the
  [23Xi[123X-th  copy. For [23Xp[123X-groups it is the largest [23Xp[123X-quotient of the quotient of the
  free product of [23Xn[123X copies of [23XH[123X by the defining relations; for perfect groups,

  [1X4.2 [33X[0;0YThe hybrid group Q(H)[133X[101X
  
  [33X[0;0YThe  iterated  group  [23X\chi^2(H)  =  \chi(K)[123X, [23XK = \chi(H) = \langle H, H^\psi
  \rangle[123X,  is generated by four copies [23XH, H^\psi, H^{\psi'}, H^{\psi\psi'}[123X of
  [23XH[123X,  as  is  [23X\chi_4(H)[123X.  Four  of  the  six  weak  commutativity relations of
  [23X\chi_4(H)[123X  hold in [23X\chi^2(H)[123X; the hybrid group is [23XQ(H) = \chi^2(H)/N_0[123X, with
  [23XN_0[123X  the normal closure of [23X[h, h^{\psi\psi'}][123X and [23X[h^\psi, h^{\psi'}][123X, [23Xh \in
  H[123X:  the  largest  common quotient of [23X\chi^2(H)[123X and [23X\chi_4(H)[123X [SRd26, Section
  7].[133X

  [10XEmbedding([110X[3XQ[103X[10X,  i)[110X,  [23X1  \leq  i  \leq  4[123X,  are  the  embeddings  of [23XH, H^\psi,

  [1X4.3 [33X[0;0YDiagonal representations from Hamming codes[133X[101X
  
  [33X[0;0YLet  [23XM_n[123X be the parity check matrix of the binary Hamming code of length [23X2^n
  -  1[123X, built recursively: [23XM_1 = [1][123X, and [23XM_{n+1}[123X has the rows of [23XM_n[123X followed
  by  [23X0[123X  and  again  by the same row of [23XM_n[123X, and a last row with [23X2^n - 1[123X zeros
  followed   by   [23X2^n[123X   ones,   whose  columns  are  the  nonzero  vectors  of
  [23X\mathbb{F}_2^n[123X.  Sending  the copy [23Xh^{\psi_{i-1}}[123X to the element of [23XH^{2^n -
  1}[123X  obtained  from the [23Xi[123X-th row of [23XM_n[123X (with [23Xh[123X for [23X1[123X and the identity for [23X0[123X)
  defines  a  homomorphism  [23X\rho_n  :  \chi_n(H)  \to  H^{2^n - 1}[123X whose image
  contains  [23X\gamma_n(H)^{2^n  -  1}[123X; if [23XH[123X is perfect, [23X\rho_n[123X is surjective (D.
  Savchuk, appendix to [SRd26]).[133X
