  [1X3.1 [33X[0;0YConstruction[133X[101X
  
  [33X[0;0YLet  [23XG[123X  be  a  group  and  [23XG^\psi[123X an isomorphic copy of [23XG[123X, with [23X\psi : G \to
  G^\psi[123X an isomorphism. Sidki's weak commutativity group is[133X
  
  
  [24X[33X[0;6Y\chi(G) = \langle G, G^\psi \mid [g, g^\psi] = 1 \ \forall g \in G \rangle.[133X
  
  [124X
  
  [33X[0;0YHere  it  is  computed  as  the  quotient of the free product [23XG * G[123X by these
  relations, keeping the two embeddings of [23XG[123X.[133X

  [1X3.4 [33X[0;0YReduced presentations: weak commutativity on products of generators[133X[101X
  
  [33X[0;0YLet  [23XS[123X  be  a  generating  set  of [23XG[123X and [23Xm \geq 1[123X. Oliveira and Sidki [OS09,
  Section 4] define[133X
  
  
  [24X[33X[0;6Y\chi(G,  S;  m)  =  \langle G, G^\psi \mid [a, a^\psi] = 1 \ \forall a \in S
  \cup S^2 \cup \cdots \cup S^m \rangle,[133X
  
  [124X
  
  [33X[0;0Ywhere [23XS^i[123X is the set of products of [23Xi[123X elements of [23XS[123X. There are two copies of
  [23XG[123X,  as  in [23X\chi(G)[123X, and [23Xm[123X bounds the length of the products; this is not the
  group  [23X\chi_n(H)[123X  with  [23Xn[123X  copies  of [2XSidkiChiMotherGroup[102X ([14X4.1-2[114X). The group
  [23X\chi(G)[123X is a quotient of [23X\chi(G, S; m)[123X, and equal to it when [23Xm[123X is large.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YThe group [23X\chi(G, S; m)[123X may be infinite for finite [23XG[123X: for [23XG = C_2 \times C_2[123X
  and [23XS = \{a_1, a_2\}[123X, [23X\chi(G, S; 1)[123X contains a free abelian group of rank [23X2[123X;
  and  for  [23XG  = C_2^3[123X the kernel of [23X\chi(G, S; 2) \to \chi(G)[123X is free abelian
  (Theorem  8).  On  the other hand, [23X\chi(G, S; 2) = \chi(G)[123X when [23XG[123X is abelian
  and generated by two elements (Corollary 1), and when [23XG[123X is finite abelian of
  odd  order  (Corollary  2).  So  the  package returns the finitely presented
  group,  with  the  embeddings  of  the  two  copies  and the projection onto
  [23X\chi(G)[123X,  also  finitely  presented;  quotients can be computed from it, for
  instance by [10XEpimorphismPGroup[110X with a bound on the class.[133X
