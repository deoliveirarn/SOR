  
  [1X1 [33X[0;0YIntroduction[133X[101X
  
  [33X[0;0YThe  name  [5XSOR[105X  stands for Sidki, de Oliveira and Rocco, whose constructions
  the  package  computes:  Sidki's  weak  commutativity group [23X\chi(G)[123X [Sid80],
  Rocco's  group  [23X\nu(G)[123X  [Roc91], and the groups with [23Xn[123X copies [SRd26] and by
  bijections [OS09] of de Oliveira and his coauthors.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YThe  package  rests  on  these  works: Sidki [Sid80] (the group [23X\chi(G)[123X, its
  subgroups  and  the  group  [23XT(G)  \le  G  \times  G \times G[123X); Rocco [Roc82]
  ([23XW(G)/R(G)  \cong  M(G)[123X); Gupta, Rocco and Sidki [GRS86]; Rocco [Roc91] (the
  group  [23X\nu(G)[123X and the non-abelian tensor square) and [Roc94] (a presentation
  of  [23X\nu(G)[123X  for finite solvable groups); Dias and Rocco [DR20] (a polycyclic
  presentation  for  the  [23Xq[123X-tensor square of a polycyclic group, and the group
  [23X\tau^q(G)[123X);  Eick  and  Nickel  [EN08]  (the  non-abelian tensor square of a
  polycyclic  group,  in  the  [5XPolycyclic[105X  package);  Kochloukova and Mendonça
  [KM21];  Oliveira  and  Sidki [OS09]; Bastos, de Oliveira, Monetta and Rocco
  [BdMR22]; Bastos, de Oliveira and Rocha Ortega [BdR26]; and Sidki, Rocco and
  de Oliveira [SRd26]. The code goes back to the author's doctoral thesis [{de
  }07], supervised by Sidki.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YThe  [5XSOR[105X  package  computes Rocco's group [23X\nu(G)[123X and the groups [23X\tau^q(G)[123X of
  Dias  and  Rocco,  Sidki's  weak  commutativity  group  [23X\chi(G)[123X,  the groups
  [23X\chi(n,H)[123X  and  [23X\chi_n(H)[123X with [23Xn[123X copies, and the hybrid group [23XQ(H)[123X, together
  with  their  subgroups,  and the groups [23X\chi(H,\sigma)[123X of weak commutativity
  defined  by  a  bijection, with the reductions of Oliveira and Sidki. All of
  them  are  quotients  of the free product of copies of [23XG[123X, so the input group
  may be given in any representation (permutation, pc, pcp, matrix or finitely
  presented) and the embeddings of the copies of [23XG[123X are kept.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YThe  key point of the package is that these embeddings are kept (see Chapter
  [14X2[114X):  the  subgroups  [23XD[123X,  [23XL[123X, [23XW[123X, [23XR[123X of [23X\chi(G)[123X and their analogues in the other
  groups  become  direct computations, and the input group may be given in any
  representation.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YThis  is  a  preliminary  version.  Planned for later versions: a polycyclic
  presentation of [23X\nu^q(G)[123X itself, and [23X\nu(G)[123X for non-solvable groups.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YThe  package  is  a  compilation  of  the  [5XGAP[105X code that the author has been
  writing  since  his  doctoral studies under the supervision of Said N. Sidki
  (Universidade  de  Brasília,  2007),  and  that  he has used in his research
  papers.  The  original  functions  are  kept  in  the folder [11Xoriginal[111X of the
  package,  unchanged  except for a first line giving their date; they are not
  loaded.[133X
