1 Introduction

The name SOR stands for Sidki, de Oliveira and Rocco, whose constructions the package computes: Sidki's weak commutativity group \(\chi(G)\) [Sid80], Rocco's group \(\nu(G)\) [Roc91], and the groups with \(n\) copies [SRd26] and by bijections [OS09] of de Oliveira and his coauthors.

The package rests on these works: Sidki [Sid80] (the group \(\chi(G)\), its subgroups and the group \(T(G) \le G \times G \times G\)); Rocco [Roc82] (\(W(G)/R(G) \cong M(G)\)); Gupta, Rocco and Sidki [GRS86]; Rocco [Roc91] (the group \(\nu(G)\) and the non-abelian tensor square) and [Roc94] (a presentation of \(\nu(G)\) for finite solvable groups); Dias and Rocco [DR20] (a polycyclic presentation for the \(q\)-tensor square of a polycyclic group, and the group \(\tau^q(G)\)); Eick and Nickel [EN08] (the non-abelian tensor square of a polycyclic group, in the Polycyclic package); Kochloukova and Mendonça [KM21]; Oliveira and Sidki [OS09]; Bastos, de Oliveira, Monetta and Rocco [BdMR22]; Bastos, de Oliveira and Rocha Ortega [BdR26]; and Sidki, Rocco and de Oliveira [SRd26]. The code goes back to the author's doctoral thesis [{de }07], supervised by Sidki.

The SOR package computes Rocco's group \(\nu(G)\) and the groups \(\tau^q(G)\) of Dias and Rocco, Sidki's weak commutativity group \(\chi(G)\), the groups \(\chi(n,H)\) and \(\chi_n(H)\) with \(n\) copies, and the hybrid group \(Q(H)\), together with their subgroups, and the groups \(\chi(H,\sigma)\) of weak commutativity defined by a bijection, with the reductions of Oliveira and Sidki. All of them are quotients of the free product of copies of \(G\), so the input group may be given in any representation (permutation, pc, pcp, matrix or finitely presented) and the embeddings of the copies of \(G\) are kept.

The key point of the package is that these embeddings are kept (see Chapter 2): the subgroups \(D\), \(L\), \(W\), \(R\) of \(\chi(G)\) and their analogues in the other groups become direct computations, and the input group may be given in any representation.

This is a preliminary version. Planned for later versions: a polycyclic presentation of \(\nu^q(G)\) itself, and \(\nu(G)\) for non-solvable groups.

The package is a compilation of the GAP code that the author has been writing since his doctoral studies under the supervision of Said N. Sidki (Universidade de Brasília, 2007), and that he has used in his research papers. The original functions are kept in the folder original of the package, unchanged except for a first line giving their date; they are not loaded.
