  [1X5.1 [33X[0;0YConstruction[133X[101X
  
  [33X[0;0YLet [23XG^\varphi[123X be an isomorphic copy of [23XG[123X. Rocco's group is[133X
  
  
  [24X[33X[0;6Y\nu(G) = \langle G, G^\varphi \mid [x, y^\varphi]^z = [x^z, (y^z)^\varphi] =
  [x, y^\varphi]^{z^\varphi} \ \forall x, y, z \in G \rangle,[133X
  
  [124X
  
  [33X[0;0Yand  the  subgroup [23X[G, G^\varphi][123X of [23X\nu(G)[123X is isomorphic to the non-abelian
  tensor square [23XG \otimes G[123X [Roc91].[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YTwo methods are used, both keeping the embeddings of [23XG[123X and [23XG^\varphi[123X:[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Yfinite  [23Xp[123X-groups:  the  quotient  of  the  free  product  [23XG * G[123X by the
        relations  of  Rocco  [Roc94]  for an AG-system [23Xa_1, \ldots, a_n[123X of [23XG[123X,
        [23X[a_i,  a_j^\varphi]^{a_k}  =  [a_i^{a_k}, (a_j^{a_k})^\varphi] = [a_i,
        a_j^\varphi]^{a_k^\varphi}[123X, followed by its largest [23Xp[123X-quotient;[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Yother  finite  solvable  groups  and  polycyclic  groups: the function
        [10XNonAbelianTensorSquarePlus[110X  of  the  [5XPolycyclic[105X  package [EN08], whose
        first  [23X2n[123X  generators  are  the images of the [23Xn[123X generators of [23XG[123X and of
        [23XG^\varphi[123X.[133X
