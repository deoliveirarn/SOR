  [1X7.1 [33X[0;0YConstruction[133X[101X
  
  [33X[0;0YLet  [23XH[123X  be a finite group, [23X\dot H[123X an isomorphic copy of [23XH[123X, and [23X\sigma : H^\#
  \to  \dot  H^\#[123X  a  bijection,  where  [23XH^\#  =  H  \setminus \{1\}[123X. The weak
  commutativity group defined by [23X\sigma[123X is[133X
  
  
  [24X[33X[0;6Y\chi(H,\sigma)  =  \langle  H, \dot H \mid [h, h^\sigma] = 1 \ \forall h \in
  H^\# \rangle[133X
  
  [124X
  
  [33X[0;0Y[OS09].  When  [23X\sigma[123X  is the isomorphism [23X\psi[123X, it is Sidki's group [23X\chi(H)[123X.
  The  group  [23X\chi(H,\sigma)[123X  is  the group [23XS(H, \dot H; \sigma, \sigma, 1)[123X of
  Sidki  [Sid80,  Theorem  3.5  and  Remark 3.3 (iii)], hence it is finite for
  every finite [23XH[123X. The package computes it exactly, by coset enumeration in the
  finitely  presented  group, and returns a pc group when it is solvable and a
  permutation group otherwise.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YOliveira and Sidki [OS09] conjecture that [23X\chi(H,\sigma)[123X is a finite [23Xp[123X-group
  whenever  [23XH[123X  is a finite [23Xp[123X-group. This is open, so the package does not take
  the  largest  [23Xp[123X-quotient  for  the  group:  [10XIsPGroup[110X of the result tests the
  conjecture for the given [23X\sigma[123X.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YA  bijection [23X\sigma[123X is given by a permutation [23Xs[123X of [23X[1 .. |H| - 1][123X: if [23XH^\# =
  [  h_1,  \ldots,  h_{|H|-1}  ][123X is the list [2XSidkiNonIdentityElements[102X ([14X7.1-1[114X),
  then  [23X\sigma(h_i)[123X  is  the  copy  of [23Xh_{i^s}[123X. The identity permutation gives
  [23X\chi(H)[123X.[133X

  [1X7.2 [33X[0;0YReductions (Oliveira and Sidki, 2009)[133X[101X
  
  [33X[0;0YFor   [23X\alpha   \in  \mathrm{Aut}(H)[123X  and  [23X\beta  \in  \mathrm{Aut}(\dot  H)[123X,
  [23X\chi(H,\sigma)  \cong  \chi(H,  \alpha\sigma\beta)[123X;  so  it  suffices to let
  [23X\sigma[123X    run    over   the   double   cosets   [23X\mathrm{Aut}(H)   \backslash
  \mathrm{Sym}(H^\#)  /  \mathrm{Aut}(H)[123X.  For an elementary abelian group [23XH =
  C_p^n   =   \langle  a_1,  \ldots,  a_n  \rangle[123X,  every  class  contains  a
  [23X\tilde\sigma[123X  with  [23Xa_i^{\tilde\sigma}  =  \dot  a_i[123X;  and, for [23Xp[123X odd, every
  [23X\chi(H,\sigma)[123X  is  a  quotient  of  some [23X\chi(H,\hat\sigma)[123X with [23X\hat\sigma[123X
  permuting  the  cyclic  subgroups of [23XH[123X (Sidki). For cyclic [23XH[123X, [23X\chi(H,\sigma)
  \cong H \times \dot H[123X for every [23X\sigma[123X (Oliveira and Sidki 2009, Proposition
  5).[133X
